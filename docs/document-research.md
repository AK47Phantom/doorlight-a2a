# Why The Demo Documents Look Different

Doorlight's source packet is original fictional content. It is **not** a set of California forms and must never be used to conduct a transaction.

The layouts follow broad record families observed in authoritative examples:

- agreements use numbered provisions and a signed audit trail;
- amendments isolate the term being changed;
- lender decisions use correspondence and condition registers;
- loan/cost records use amount tables and comparisons;
- seller disclosures use completed responses and continuations;
- visual/property records use room or finding numbers and access limits;
- title records use schedules and numbered exceptions;
- settlement records use instructions and debit/credit ledgers;
- insurance records use coverage schedules and issuer acknowledgments.

Every fixture contains transaction-specific facts mixed with relevant surrounding language. Every execution field is populated with a clearly labeled simulated electronic entry, an explicit pending state, or an explicit missing state. The Red case therefore does not gain a seller signature merely to make the page look complete.

The main references were the [California DRE disclosure example](https://www.dre.ca.gov/files/pdf/re6.pdf), [CFPB Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure samples](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/mortgage-resources/tila-respa-integrated-disclosures/forms-samples/), [California Structural Pest Control Board WDO report](https://www.pestboard.ca.gov/forms/43m-41-short.pdf), [EPA lead disclosure sample](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/documents/selr_eng.pdf), [Fannie Mae Form 1004](https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/document/pdf/form-1004), and a [historical C.A.R. AVID anatomy reference](https://www.car.org/-/media/CAR/Documents/Transaction-Center/PDF/Standard-Forms/June-Releases/AVID_6-19_markup_web.pdf). The implementation paraphrases structural ideas and does not reproduce protected wording or branding.

## Honest demo boundary

Dense documents make the demonstration more demanding; they do not prove legal completeness, OCR accuracy on arbitrary uploads, or production scalability. Preserved text is currently authoritative, while local vision/OCR assists intake. A real deployment would ingest customer-controlled originals, store page coordinates, benchmark OCR and extraction against labeled samples, and have qualified California professionals maintain the required-form and policy set.
